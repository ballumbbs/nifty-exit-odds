Cost = buy price − sell price when the spread opened. Value = the same at exit. P&L = (value − cost) × lot size − charges. Prices are recorded by a run every 5 minutes, so real fills can differ a little.

The rules

  1. Opening range = the 09:15–09:30 candle (15-minute chart).
  2. 09:30–11:30: a 15-minute candle closes above the range (BULL) or below it (BEAR), on the same side of VWAP, with ADX above 20. One trade a day. Tuesdays (expiry) are skipped.
  3. BULL: buy the ATM call, sell the call 150 points higher. BEAR: buy the ATM put, sell the put 150 points lower.
  4. Exit when a candle closes back inside the range, Nifty reaches the other side of the range, Nifty moves 100 points in your favour, or at 15:00.
